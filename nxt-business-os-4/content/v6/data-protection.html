Zimbabwe / Data protection

Working under Zimbabwe’s data protection framework

Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] designates POTRAZ as the Data Protection Authority. When your organisation decides why and how personal data is used, it is the data controller. When NXT handles those records on your instructions, it acts as a data processor.

Before any records move, we agree a written processing arrangement covering authorised access, security measures, retention, return or deletion, and incident escalation. We document where data will be hosted or accessed and identify any cross-border transfer requirements.

During scoping, we identify the licensing, registration and data protection officer requirements your organisation needs to check under Statutory Instrument 155 of 2024, including any applicable exemption. Your nominated lead retains responsibility for the controller’s decisions and notifications.

We escalate a suspected incident to your nominated contact immediately. Section 19 of the Act requires the controller to notify POTRAZ within 24 hours of a security breach affecting data it processes.
