What are you looking for?

Managed services / Data protection

Monthly data protection support.

Give your team a consistent way to handle customer and employee records. NXT works to your written instructions and helps you keep track of who can access the data, what changed and what needs attention.

Request a monthly plan
At onboarding

Agree the scope

We map the records, systems and people involved. Together we set access permissions, retention periods, approved storage locations and a secure way to share records before work begins.

During the month

Handle the records

We organise and update agreed records, process authorised corrections and deletions, and keep a log of requests. If we suspect unauthorised access, we escalate it immediately to your nominated contact.

At month end

Review the work

You receive a summary of completed requests, access changes, retention actions and outstanding decisions. Your monthly fee is quoted around the volume of records, the systems involved and the work you need.

South Africa / POPIA

Who is responsible for the data?

When your organisation decides why and how personal information is used, it remains the responsible party. When NXT handles those records on your behalf, our role is the operator. We agree the safeguards, confidentiality and incident reporting process in a written agreement. Before any records are accessed across borders, we agree where processing will take place and the applicable transfer safeguards.
