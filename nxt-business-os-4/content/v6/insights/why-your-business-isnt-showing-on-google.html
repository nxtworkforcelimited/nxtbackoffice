Why Your Business Is Not Showing on Google

Seven practical reasons customers may not be finding you in search, and what to fix first.

6 Oct 2026 • NXT Workforce

Your Google Business Profile may be incomplete6. You are not publishing useful answers7. Nobody credible is linking to youA quick search visibility checklist

1. Google may not know your pages exist

Set up Google Search Console, confirm your site is indexed and submit a sitemap. If Google cannot discover the pages, no amount of good copy will help them rank.

2. Your page titles may be too vague

A page called Home or Services does not explain what the page is about. Use clear titles that combine the service, business or location where it makes sense.

3. One page is trying to rank for everything

Give important services their own pages. A separate page for each service gives search engines a clearer topic and gives customers a better answer.

4. Mobile performance may be working against you

Test the site on a real phone. Large images, heavy plugins and unnecessary animation can make pages slow and frustrate visitors before they contact you.

5. Your Google Business Profile may be incomplete

Keep your address, phone, hours, categories, services and photographs current. For local searches, this profile can be as important as the website.

6. You are not publishing useful answers

Create articles that answer the questions customers ask before buying. Useful content gives search engines more context and gives people a reason to trust you.

7. Nobody credible is linking to you

Relevant directory listings, industry bodies, suppliers, partners and local media can strengthen trust signals. Focus on relevant, reputable links rather than buying large quantities of poor-quality links.

A quick search visibility checklist

  • Search Console is connected
  • A sitemap is submitted
  • Every major service has its own page
  • Page titles say what the page is about
  • Your mobile site loads quickly
  • Google Business Profile details are complete
  • You publish useful business content
  • Your contact details are consistent across the web
